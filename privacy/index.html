MAXX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 1.3

This policy explains what MAXX collects, what it does not, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

1. Who we are

MAXX is operated by Kaotik Games, based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy, contact us at kaotikgamesapp@gmail.com.

2. The short version

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. You can use MAXX without creating an account in the usual sense.

Your photos are not stored. They are sent for analysis, the result is returned, and the image is discarded. We keep only how many images you sent and their total size.

Your practice chats are kept for 30 days, then deleted. The messages you write in the practice simulator, the character's replies and the coach's notes are stored on our server so you can rewind, try a different line and reopen a conversation. They are deleted automatically 30 days after you last used that conversation, and immediately if you delete your account.

4. Photos — in detail

This is the part people care about most, so here is exactly what happens.

  1. You choose a photo. It is uploaded to our server over an encrypted connection.
  2. Our server checks it is a valid image and passes it to an AI provider (§5).
  3. The AI returns a text result. That result is saved to your account.
  4. The image itself is not written to disk and not kept. It exists only in memory during processing.

Because of this, we cannot show you your old photos, and we cannot recover them. That is intentional.

4.1 Microphone and speech recognition — voice dates

Voice dates use your microphone and speech recognition only with your permission. Your speech is converted to text on your device or by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (operating system provider). On-device recognition is preferred; if it is unavailable or the language is unsupported, your phone's service may process your audio over the network under the operating system provider's privacy policy. MAXX never receives or stores your audio. For each voice turn, only the transcript and speaking and silence durations are sent to our server over an encrypted connection. The transcript is processed by third-party AI services, like written practice messages, to generate replies and feedback.

The character's reply text is sent to a third-party speech generation service. The generated character audio is held briefly in server memory for playback; it is not saved to disk or our database. This is synthetic character speech, not a recording of your voice. If server speech is unavailable, the device can read the reply.

You can withdraw microphone access at any time in your device settings. On iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → MAXX and turn it off; do the same under Speech Recognition. On Android, open Settings → Apps → MAXX → Permissions → Microphone → Don't allow. Menu labels vary by device. You can continue by typing if permissions are denied, withdrawn or speech recognition is unavailable. Revoking a permission stops future microphone use; delete your account to remove existing transcripts immediately.

4.2 Chat twin — a profile from your chat screenshot

When you choose to build a chat twin, we use the transcript extracted from the chat screenshot you uploaded to create a profile of the other person's observed texting style: message length, emoji use, tone and an estimated interest level. The profile contains no names or direct quotes. It is a fictional rehearsal aid, not a statement of the person's thoughts or a prediction of their reply. For rehearsals and linked message tests, the original transcript, profile, your draft and prior rehearsal context are sent to third-party AI services again to generate replies, feedback and test results.

The screenshot itself is discarded after analysis. Its transcript remains in the original analysis result until account deletion; we do not store a second copy for the twin, and a source analysis older than the twin retention limit cannot be reused. Drafts and up to the last six rehearsal turns (12 messages) are held temporarily while the screen is open, not saved persistently on your device, and sent for processing when you rehearse or test. We do not save these inputs separately in our database, but generated replies, feedback and test results may quote messages.

Access to the style profile and generated twin and linked test content, including any quotes, expires no later than 30 days after each record was created (earlier if a shorter retention period is configured). Further use does not extend this period. Expired content cannot be reopened or reused. It is cleared from our database by the periodic clean-up, which runs every six hours by default, so removal can lag by a few hours. Credit and cost records remain without this content. Deleting your account removes the profile, transcript and generated content immediately.

You are responsible for ensuring you are entitled to upload and process the other person's personal data, including obtaining any necessary permission. Before uploading, we recommend obscuring names, phone numbers and other identifying details in screenshots and avoiding unnecessary personal information.

Our server never sends messages to anyone on your behalf. If you choose to share a tested draft, sharing starts from your device's system share sheet; you choose the app and recipient and confirm sending there.

6. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK applies, we process your data because it is necessary to perform the service you asked for (running an analysis, tracking your credits), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service working, secure and free of abuse. Purchase records are kept because we are legally required to keep them.

7. How long we keep things

  • Uploaded photos: not kept. Text typed into tools is not saved as a separate input; generated results may quote it (§4.2).
  • Practice simulator conversations (your messages, the character's replies, coach notes): 30 days after your last activity in that conversation, then deleted automatically. The clean-up runs several times a day, so deletion can lag by a few hours. A conversation you opened but never wrote in is removed within about a day.
  • Voice date transcripts and per-turn speaking/silence timings: the same 30-day period after your last activity in that conversation, with the same clean-up schedule. Account deletion removes them immediately. Aggregate speaking metrics in round reports remain until account deletion; direct transcript quotes in reports are removed after 30 days. Recordings of your voice are not collected. Generated character audio is only cached briefly in memory for playback.
  • Chat twin profiles and generated twin/linked test content: access expires within 30 days of each record's creation; content is then cleared by periodic clean-up (§4.2). Account deletion removes it immediately. The original screenshot transcript remains in the source analysis result until account deletion.
  • Practice progress and character memory notes: until you delete your account.
  • Other results, credits, usage events: until you delete your account. In practice round reports, the quote of your own message is removed after 30 days.
  • Purchase records: retained after account deletion, for as long as accounting and tax law requires. These records are no longer linked to any way of identifying you — the login credentials are destroyed.

8. Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete my account in the app. This is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your login identity, your results (including chat twin profiles, source transcripts and generated twin/linked test content), your practice conversations and progress, your usage history and any remaining credits, which are forfeited.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by your platform's app store. Cancel yours in that store's subscription settings, otherwise billing continues.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you may object to processing. The fastest route for deletion is the in-app button above. For anything else, write to kaotikgamesapp@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Because we do not hold your name or email, we may only be able to act on requests made from inside the app, where your identity can be established.

If you are in the EU or Türkiye you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

10. Children

MAXX is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to use it, and the app asks you to confirm this before anything else. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used MAXX, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Your session token is stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android), not in plain text. Your device identifier is stored on our side only as a one-way hash. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not keep the data that would be most damaging to lose — your photos — and practice conversations are deleted after 30 days.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the version number above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
