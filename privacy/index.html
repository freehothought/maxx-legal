MAXX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what MAXX collects, what it does not, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

1. Who we are

MAXX is operated by Kaotik Games, based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy, contact us at sercanserin@gmail.com.

2. The short version

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. You can use MAXX without creating an account in the usual sense.

Your photos are not stored. They are sent for analysis, the result is returned, and the image is discarded. We keep only how many images you sent and their total size.

4. Photos — in detail

This is the part people care about most, so here is exactly what happens.

  1. You choose a photo. It is uploaded to our server over an encrypted connection.
  2. Our server checks it is a valid image and passes it to an AI provider (§5).
  3. The AI returns a text result. That result is saved to your account.
  4. The image itself is not written to disk and not kept. It exists only in memory during processing.

Because of this, we cannot show you your old photos, and we cannot recover them. That is intentional.

6. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK applies, we process your data because it is necessary to perform the service you asked for (running an analysis, tracking your credits), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service working, secure and free of abuse. Purchase records are kept because we are legally required to keep them.

7. How long we keep things

  • Uploaded photos and typed text: not kept at all.
  • Results, credits, usage events: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: retained after account deletion, for as long as accounting and tax law requires. These records are no longer linked to any way of identifying you — the login credentials are destroyed.

8. Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete my account in the app. This is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your login identity, your results, your usage history and any remaining credits, which are forfeited.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us. Cancel yours in your App Store or Google Play account settings, otherwise billing continues.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you may object to processing. The fastest route for deletion is the in-app button above. For anything else, write to sercanserin@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Because we do not hold your name or email, we may only be able to act on requests made from inside the app, where your identity can be established.

If you are in the EU or Türkiye you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

10. Children

MAXX is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to use it, and the app asks you to confirm this before anything else. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used MAXX, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Your session token is stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android), not in plain text. Your device identifier is stored on our side only as a one-way hash. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not keep the data that would be most damaging to lose — your photos.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the version number above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
