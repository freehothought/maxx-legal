MAXX — Delete your account

Kaotik Games · applies to the MAXX app on iOS and Android

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open MAXX and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to sercanserin@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my account". Include your device model and, if you signed in with Apple or Google, the account you used. We delete within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your login identity (device ID hash, Apple / Google sign-in link)
  • Your analysis results and usage history
  • Your remaining credits — they are forfeited

What is kept, and for how long

DataWhyHow long
Purchase records (store transaction ID, amount, date)Tax and accounting law; refund disputesAs long as accounting and tax law requires (Türkiye: 10 years). Not linked to any way of identifying you once the login identity is destroyed.

Photos are never stored in the first place — they are analysed and discarded on the spot.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel yours in your App Store or Google Play account settings, otherwise billing continues.
